\section{Methodology \& Rough Processing Steps (Pipeline Overview)}
This research examines the microstructure of 10 Nifty 50 stocks (5 liquid, 5 illiquid) and their corresponding futures contracts on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) during the final 30-minute settlement window (15:00--15:30 IST) across 12 Monthly Expiry Thursdays and 12 matched Control trading days in 2022. Below is the step-by-step processing workflow:
\vspace{0.2cm}
\subsection*{Step 1: Sample Selection \& Experimental Design (10 Symbols x 24 Dates)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item Target Asset Universe: Select 5 Liquid Nifty 50 stocks (RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK) and 5 Illiquid Nifty 50 stocks (GRASIM, HEROMOTOCO, SHREEVECEM, UPL, TECHM).
  \item Experimental Pairing: Match 12 Monthly Expiry Thursdays in 2022 against 12 Control Thursdays (typically 2 weeks prior to expiry) to isolate expiry-day effects from day-of-week seasonality.
  \item Settlement Focus Window: Extract tick-by-tick cash market and derivatives market data during the NSE VWAP settlement window (15:00 to 15:30 IST).
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 2: High-Speed Binary Tick Data Parsing (Stage 1)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item Input Data Format: Ingest fixed-width binary text files (.DAT.gz) from NSE for CASH\_Orders, CASH\_Trades, FAO\_Orders, and FAO\_Trades.
  \item Predicate Pushdown: Filter raw strings on symbol substrings and instrument types ('EQ' for Cash, 'FUTSTK' for Futures) before extracting schema columns.
  \item Column Extraction: Extract 17 standardized fields including Order/Trade numbers, timestamps (Jiffies), prices, volumes, client identity, and algo indicator flags, saving as partitioned Parquet.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 3: Temporal \& Microstructure Enrichment (Stage 2)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item Timestamp Normalization: Convert NSE Jiffies (1/65536th of a second since epoch) into standard Python/Spark timestamps and HH:mm:ss time buckets.
  \item Currency \& Flag Mapping: Convert Paise to INR Rupees (/ 100.0), flag the 30-minute settlement window (15:00-15:30 IST), and identify Expiry vs. Control dates.
  \item Participant \& Algo Tagging: Categorize Client Identity into Custodian (Institutional), Proprietary, and NCNP (Retail), and classify Algo Indicator flags into Algo vs. Non-Algo execution.
  \item Dynamic Partitioning: Save enriched datasets partitioned by ['symbol', 'trade\_date'] to enable instant single-symbol filter pushdown during downstream CLOB replay.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 4: Trade-Level Microstructure Metrics Computation (Stage 3)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item A1 (VWAP Trajectory): Compute cumulative and rolling 1-minute Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP) trajectories across 15:00-15:30 IST.
  \item A2 (Basis Divergence): Compute intraday Futures-Cash basis spread and Basis Volatility (standard deviation of basis).
  \item A3-A7 (Participant \& Algo Profiling): Quantify Prop/Custodian trade shares, Algo volume contribution, Immediate-Or-Cancel (IOC) aggressiveness, cancellation-to-trade ratios, and iceberg hidden order volume.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 5: High-Performance Limit Order Book (CLOB) Reconstruction (Stage 4)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item Chronological Event Merging: Merge order entry/modify/cancel events with trade execution events into a unified chronological stream.
  \item Data-Oriented Design (C++): Replay events through a cache-line aligned C++ order book engine (FlatPriceBook with O(log N) price level lookup and zero heap fragmentation).
  \item 1-Second Depth Snapshots: Emit Top-10 bid/ask price levels and available shares at every second from 15:00:00 to 15:30:00 IST (1,800 snapshots per symbol/date).
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 6: Limit Order Book Liquidity \& Price Impact Analysis (Stage 5)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item B1-B2 (Spread \& Depth Dynamics): Calculate percentage bid-ask spread widening and Top-5 / Top-10 order book depth erosion across the settlement window.
  \item B3-B5 (OFI, Kyle's Lambda \& Asymmetry): Calculate Order Flow Imbalance (OFI), estimate Kyle's Lambda (price impact per million INR traded via OLS regression), and measure Bid-vs-Ask book asymmetry.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 7: Bloomberg Terminal Integration \& Cross-Market Validation (Stage 6)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item C1 (Roll Pressure): Ingest Bloomberg calendar spread data to quantify institutional rollover urgency and predict net directional punch ('UP' or 'DOWN').
  \item C2-C3 (Cost-of-Carry \& Cross-Validation): Calculate implied repo rates and test whether Bloomberg roll direction predicts actual NSE Cash VWAP terminal drift and CLOB book imbalance.
  \item C4 (Event Study): Evaluate price trajectories around block trade executions during the final 15 minutes.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 8: Formal Hypothesis Testing Engine \& Statistical Reporting (Stage 7)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item Statistical Test Execution: Evaluate all 30 formal hypotheses (H1-H30) using Paired t-tests, Wilcoxon Signed-Rank tests, Mann-Whitney U tests, OLS regression, Spearman rank correlation, and Binomial tests.
  \item FDR Multiple-Testing Correction: Apply Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate correction across all p-values to control Type I errors.
  \item Artifact Compilation: Automatically generate publication-quality figures (fig1 to fig10), summary statistical tables, and the final Markdown research paper.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.15cm}
\subsection*{Step 9: Modular Staged Execution \& Scale-Up Strategy}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=2pt]
  \item Staged Workflow: Start processing with 1 or 2 symbols/dates for rapid initial verification of the entire pipeline.
  \item Incremental Scaling: Copy additional raw data files into 'data/raw/' at any time; pipeline automatically skips previously parsed/built snapshots and dynamically updates partitions without wiping existing data.
\end{itemize}
