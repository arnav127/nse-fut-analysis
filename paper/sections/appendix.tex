\section{All measures, by broad hypothesis}\label{app:tests}

Every hypothesis of the original design is retained as a measure of one of the four broad
hypotheses. Each is a paired $t$-test of the expiry session against the control session of the
same month, one pair per security. The primary measure of each broad hypothesis is marked P;
the others are supporting evidence and are not used to accept or reject the broad hypothesis.
