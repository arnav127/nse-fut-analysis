\section{All measures, by broad hypothesis}\label{app:tests}

The table reports every measure, grouped under the broad hypothesis it belongs to. Each is a
paired $t$-test of the expiry session against the control session of the same month, one pair
per security. The primary measure of each broad hypothesis is marked P; the others are
supporting evidence and are not used to accept or reject the broad hypothesis.
